Part A - Multiple Choice: Questions 1-2
Question 1 [1] CoreDoubleTriple

Which equation defines density?

A ρ = V/m   B ρ = m/V   C ρ = mV   D ρ = m + V

Answer: B. Density is mass per unit volume.
Question 2 [1] CoreDoubleTriple

Which pair of instruments is required for a small irregular stone that sinks in water?

A balance and measuring cylinder; ruler and stopwatch; spring balance and thermometer; micrometer and ammeter.

Answer: A. Use the balance for mass and water displacement for volume.
Part A - Multiple Choice: Questions 3-4
Question 3 [1] CoreDoubleTriple

A box exerts 600 N on 0.20 m². What pressure does it exert?

A 120 Pa   B 3 000 Pa   C 30 000 Pa   D 120 000 Pa

Answer: B. p = F/A = 600/0.20 = 3 000 Pa.
Question 4 [1] CoreDoubleTriple

Which statement about pressure caused by a stationary liquid is correct?

Answer: C. Liquid pressure increases with depth.
Part B - Structured Question
Question 5 [6] CoreDoubleTriple

A metal block has mass 162 g and dimensions 6.0 cm by 4.0 cm by 2.5 cm.

(a) Calculate volume. [2]

(b) Calculate density in g cm-3. [2]

(c) Describe measuring the volume of an irregular sample by water displacement. [2]

Question 5 - Worked Answer and Mark Scheme

(a) V = 6.0 × 4.0 × 2.5 = 60 cm³. Method [1], answer [1].

(b) ρ = m/V = 162/60 = 2.7 g cm-3. Substitution [1], answer [1].

(c) Record initial water volume; fully submerge sample; record final volume; subtract initial from final. Method [1], subtraction [1].

Part B - Structured Question
Question 6 [5] CoreDoubleTriple

A crate weighs 960 N and rests on a 0.80 m by 0.50 m face.

(a) Calculate contact area. [1]

(b) Calculate pressure. [3]

(c) Explain the change when it is turned onto a smaller face. [1]

Question 6 - Worked Answer and Mark Scheme

(a) A = 0.80 × 0.50 = 0.40 m². [1]

(b) p = F/A = 960/0.40 = 2 400 Pa. Relationship [1], substitution [1], answer [1].

(c) Pressure increases because the same force acts over a smaller area. [1]

Part B - Structured Question
Question 7 [6] SupplementDoubleTriple

Use g = 10 N kg-1. A point is 0.80 m below water of density 1 000 kg m-3.

(a) State effects of depth and density. [2]

(b) Calculate pressure increase. [3]

(c) Compare with denser brine at the same depth. [1]

Question 7 - Worked Answer and Mark Scheme

(a) Pressure increases with depth [1]; at the same depth it is greater in a denser liquid [1].

(b) Δp = ρgΔh = 1 000 × 10 × 0.80 = 8 000 Pa. Relationship [1], substitution [1], answer [1].

(c) Greater in brine because brine has greater density. [1]
